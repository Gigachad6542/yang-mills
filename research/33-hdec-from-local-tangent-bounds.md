# Rooted decorrelation (H-dec) from local single-step tangent bounds

Status:

- **Proved here.**
  - Lemma 1: a globally Ad-invariant analytic local function has no linear
    term at the flat field. Composed with a background map of size r, its
    oscillation is at most 2||h||(r/α)². So a gauge-invariant local insertion
    has no dimension-2 (relevant) component, and transport by one level gives
    L^{-4} on its leading part.
  - Lemma 2: a zero-sum, finitely supported coefficient field of a marginal
    operator is a lattice divergence. Its insertion is therefore a sum of
    differences O_x - O_{x+e}, a dimension-5 (irrelevant) operator, with an
    explicit ℓ¹ bound.
  - Theorem 3: (H-dec) with an explicit **polynomial** factor follows from a
    local, extensive (ℓ¹) form of the single-step tangent bounds of note 29,
    (LS), plus a large-field hypothesis (LF).
  - Corollary 4: the corresponding correction to note 32 Cor. 5. It stays
    summable, because the large-field weight w_m is superpolynomially small
    in m.
- **Correction to note 32.** Iterating a per-step sup-norm contraction
  "C L^{-4}" loses C^m. The uniform form |Cov| <= C_G L^{-4m}||h|| is not
  what single-step bounds give. What they give is L^{-4m} times
  Π_j(1 + K g_j²), a power of m. The rate L^{-4m} itself is **charge
  conservation plus volume normalization**, not a contraction.
- **Open.** (LS) and (LF) for Balaban's RG step.

## 1. Lemma 1: no relevant part

Let Y be a finite set of bonds and P_α = {a ∈ sl(2,C)^Y : |a_b| <= α for all b}.

**Lemma 1.** Let h be analytic on P_α, with |h| <= ||h|| there, and globally
Ad-invariant: h(Ad_g a) = h(a) for g ∈ SU(2), acting on all bonds at once.
Then for |a|_∞ <= r <= α,

    |h(a) - h(0)| <= 2 ||h|| (r/α)².

*Proof.*

1. Dh(0) is a real-linear functional on su(2)^Y (extended complex-linearly)
   and is Ad-invariant. Each component is an Ad-invariant vector of
   su(2)* ≅ su(2). The adjoint representation of SU(2) is irreducible and
   nontrivial, so it has no invariant vectors. Hence Dh(0) = 0.
2. Put φ(z) = h(z a) for |z| <= R := α/|a|_∞ >= 1. Then φ'(0) = Dh(0)a = 0.
3. So ψ(z) = (φ(z) - φ(0))/z² is analytic on |z| < R, and |ψ| <= 2||h||/R² on
   |z| = R. By the maximum principle,
   |φ(1) - φ(0)| = |ψ(1)| <= 2||h||/R² = 2||h||(|a|_∞/α)². ∎

**Reading.** Suppose a gauge-invariant local function at scale k is
evaluated on the fine background generated by a coarse field. In a local
axial gauge on the O(1) support of h, the background's links are of size
C_reg κ'/L², where κ' is the coarse curvature. This is the smoothness of the
minimizer on the coarse scale (CMP 102b; the fine curvature is the coarse
curvature divided by L²). Lemma 1 then gives a transported oscillation of
2C_reg²(κ'/α)² L^{-4}||h||. There is no L^{-2} term, because the
dimension-2 term would be linear in the curvature, and it is excluded by
Ad-invariance. This is the same group-theoretic fact as note 11 §6 (color
invariance at a flat exterior).

*Correction (note 35).* The size C_reg κ'/L² holds for coarse fields that are
smooth on the block scale. It does **not** hold uniformly over Balaban's
coarse small-field domain. That domain contains coarse fluxes that oscillate
at the block scale, and the exact abelian minimizer turns these into fine
fluxes of size s_L κ', with s_L ≈ 1.5, 1.1, 0.8, 0.55 for L = 2, 3, 4, 5
(not O(L^{-2})). So in the sup norm over the whole domain, Lemma 1 gives
only 2||h||(s_L κ'/α)² per level. The factor L^{-4} holds on the smooth
(low-momentum) part. This affects only this reading. Theorem 3 below does
not use it: it assumes (LS) and gets L^{-4m} from charge conservation.

**Why this does not iterate.** Applied once per level, Lemma 1 gives the
factor 2C_reg²L^{-4} per level, hence (2C_reg²)^m L^{-4m} after m levels. In
note 32 Cor. 5 the L^{-4} per level exactly cancels the L^4 placement
entropy, so any constant above 1 per level destroys summability. The
mechanism that does iterate is conservation of the marginal charge, as
follows.

## 2. Lemma 2: zero-charge redistributions are irrelevant

**Lemma 2.** Let μ: Z^d -> R be supported in a box Q of side ℓ, with
Σ_x μ(x) = 0. Then there is a vector field v = (v_1, ..., v_d) supported in
Q with

    μ(x) = Σ_ν (v_ν(x) - v_ν(x - e_ν)),   Σ_{x,ν} |v_ν(x)| <= ½ d ℓ Σ_x |μ(x)|.

Consequently, for any family of operators O_x,

    Σ_x μ(x) O_x = Σ_{x,ν} v_ν(x) (O_x - O_{x+e_ν}).   (2.1)

*Proof.*

1. Split μ = μ_+ - μ_-. Both parts have mass ½||μ||_1. Choose any coupling,
   i.e. a transport plan π(y, z) >= 0 with marginals μ_+ and μ_-.
2. For each pair (y, z), send π(y, z) units along a monotone lattice path
   in Q from z to y. Such a path changes one coordinate at a time and has
   length <= dℓ.
3. Let v be the resulting flow: the sum over pairs of π(y, z) times the
   signed indicator of the path's bonds. Its divergence is μ_+ - μ_- = μ,
   and ||v||_1 <= dℓ Σ π = ½dℓ||μ||_1.
4. (2.1) is summation by parts:
   Σ_x (v_ν(x) - v_ν(x-e_ν))O_x = Σ_x v_ν(x)(O_x - O_{x+e_ν}). ∎

**Use.** When a marginal insertion is transported one level, the induced
coarse marginal coefficients are some local field μ on coarse blocks. That
field is **not** the block average Aλ: the induced coarse quadratic form
K(x,p')K(x,q') is signed and spreads across neighboring coarse blocks.
Bounding ||μ||_1 by ||K||_{1->1}||λ||_1 compounds ||K||_{1->1} > 1 per level.

Instead, write μ = Aλ' + (μ - Aλ'), where λ' is the charge-correct part,
so that μ - Aλ' has zero total. By Lemma 2, μ - Aλ' is an insertion of
differences O_x - O_{x+e}, which are dimension 5. That part goes into the
irrelevant sector, at extensive cost <= ½dℓ ||μ - Aλ'||_1. The marginal sector
then evolves by the **exact block average** A, which is positive with
ℓ¹ norm 1.

## 3. Theorem 3

**Tangent data.** Insert s·h at level k (j = 0). Let F_j be the σ-algebra of
the level-(k+j) block variables. The s-derivative at s = 0 of the
level-(k+j) effective action is -E[h | F_j]. Modulo constants, decompose it
on the small-field set as

    -E[h | F_j] = const + Σ_x λ_j(x) O_j(x) + Σ_X ρ_{j,X},

where:

- O_j(x) is the level-j Wilson action of block x, normalized so that a
  uniform λ shifts g_{k+j}^{-2};
- ρ_{j,X} are irrelevant polymer activities. This includes the difference
  operators of Lemma 2.

Use **extensive** norms (weights per fine volume):

    n_j := L^{4j} Σ_x |λ_j(x)|,   r_j := L^{4j} Σ_X ||ρ_{j,X}||_j.

**Hypothesis (LS), local single-step tangent bounds.** For 0 <= j < m:

- (a) λ_{j+1} = Aλ_j + E_jλ_j + B_jρ_j. Here A is the exact block average,
  n(E_jλ) <= e g_j² n(λ), and n(B_jρ) <= b g_j² r(ρ).
- (b) r_{j+1} <= θ r_j + d n_j, with θ < 1.
- (c) Initial data: n_0 + r_0 <= C_0 ||h|| for h supported in O(1) blocks
  (Lemma 1 supplies the absence of a relevant part).
- (d) Coupling monotonicity: g_j² <= g_{j+1}² <= γ², and
  g_j^{-2} - g_{j+1}^{-2} >= β_* > 0 (CMP 109 (0.18), β smooth and positive
  for small γ).

Here d = O(1): the Lemma 2 redistribution is present already at tree level.
Moreover b and e are O(g²): loops generate charge from irrelevant data and
make the charge response position dependent.

**Hypothesis (LF), large fields.** For a test Ψ that is F_m-measurable with
|Ψ| <= 1,

    |Cov(Ψ, h)| <= 2 sup_{S_m} |E[h|F_m] - const| + ε_LF(m) ||h||,

where S_m is the level-m small-field set and ε_LF(m) <= C L^{-4m}.

*What (LF) contains (remark added with notes 38-39).* A crude bound
||h|| · P(large field near x at some level) does **not** give L^{-4m}. At
coarse levels near the terminal scale, the probability of a large field is
about e^{-c p(γ)²}, which does not decay with m. The L^{-4m} must instead
come from the same mechanism as for small fields. A large-field region at
level j near the insertion is a local defect. Its influence on the terminal
test is transported over m - j levels, giving L^{-4(m-j)} after averaging.
The insertion's transport up to level j gives L^{-4j}. So the expected form
is

    ε_LF(m) <= C L^{-4m} Σ_j P_j,   P_j = P(large field near x at level j).

Σ_j P_j converges by the marked bound (T2-R) of note 39. Proving this is
the large-field analogue of generalized averaged H8. It uses the R-bias
machinery of notes 25, 32 and 39, not a new kind of estimate.

**Theorem 3.** Assume (LS) and (LF), and suppose sup_{S_m}|O_m(x)| <= o and
that the polymer norms dominate sup norms. Put K := e + bd/(1-θ). Then

    |Cov(Ψ, h)| <= C_1 L^{-4m} P_m ||h|| + ε_LF(m)||h||,
    P_m := Π_{j<m} (1 + K g_j²) <= e^{Kγ²} (1 + β_* γ² m)^{K/β_*},     (3.1)

with C_1 = 2C_0[(o + d/(1-θ))(1 + bγ²/(1-θ)) + 1].

*Proof.*

1. *Conditioning.* Cov(Ψ, h) = Cov(Ψ, E[h|F_m]), because Ψ is
   F_m-measurable. Apply (LF).
2. *Terminal sup.* On S_m,
   |E[h|F_m] - const| <= o Σ_x|λ_m(x)| + Σ_X||ρ_{m,X}|| = L^{-4m}(o n_m + r_m).
   This is the only place the factor L^{-4m} enters: it is the conversion of
   an extensive norm into level-m units.
3. *Irrelevant sector.* Iterating (b), with M_j := max_{i<=j} n_i,
   r_j <= θ^j r_0 + d Σ_{i<j} θ^{j-1-i} n_i <= θ^j r_0 + (d/(1-θ)) M_{j-1}.
4. *Marginal sector.* Since n(Aλ) <= n(λ), (a) and step 3 give
   n_{j+1} <= (1 + e g_j²) n_j + b g_j² r_j <= (1 + K g_j²) M_j + b g_j² θ^j r_0.
   The right side is >= M_j, so
   M_{j+1} <= (1 + Kg_j²)M_j + bγ²θ^j r_0.
   By induction, M_m <= P_m (n_0 + bγ² r_0/(1-θ)).
5. *Collect.* o n_m + r_m <= (o + d/(1-θ))M_m + r_0. Use (c).
6. *The product.* By (d), g_{m-1-i}^{-2} >= γ^{-2} + β_* i, so
   Σ_{j<m} g_j² <= Σ_{i<m} 1/(γ^{-2} + β_* i) <= γ² + β_*^{-1} log(1 + β_*γ²m).
   Then use 1 + x <= e^x. ∎

**Remarks.**

1. *Gaussian check.* In the Gaussian model of note 19, e = b = 0, so P_m = 1
   and (3.1) is the exterior-averaged L^{-4m} found there. Fixed exteriors
   gave L^{-2m} in note 19 §5. That is consistent with Lemma 1: at a
   nonzero background the Ad-invariance argument applies only after
   averaging, so the covariance under π̂_λ is the right object.
2. *Why a power of m.* Fix one placement x. The marginal charge carried by
   its image is multiplied at each level by 1 + e(x_j), where x_j is the
   position of x inside its level-(j+1) block (its base-L digit).
   - Uniform insertion (Σ_x over positions): the charge response is
     ∂g_{j+1}^{-2}/∂g_j^{-2} = 1 + O(g⁴). So the position average of e is
     O(g⁴).
   - Individual positions: at one loop, the field-dependent part of the
     fluctuation determinant responds to a local coupling change at order
     g², with zero position average. That position-dependent part is
     generically nonzero.

   A placement whose digits repeatedly hit positions with e > 0 then
   accumulates Π(1 + cg_j²), a power of m. That is the factor P_m. This is
   a heuristic for why (3.1) need not improve to a uniform constant. It is
   not a proof that it cannot.
3. *When P_m is bounded.* If the per-position response and the feedback
   B∘D are O(g⁴), then Σ g_j⁴ < ∞ and P_m <= C. The signed, λ-summed form of
   H8_Σ (note 25 §5) can use the O(g⁴) average. The absolute-value form of
   note 32 Cor. 5 cannot.

## 4. Corollary 4: corrected note 32 Cor. 5

Insert (3.1) at insertion depth m - t into note 32 Cor. 5. Using P_{m-t} <= P_m,

    Σ_λ |T_λ| <= C Σ_m (w_m/g_m) P_m Σ_t (L⁴ C_R e^{-a})^t + Σ_m w_m L^{4m} ε_LF-terms.

This is finite under the conditions of note 32 together with

    Σ_m w_m m^{1/2 + K/β_*} < ∞,   Σ_m w_m L^{4m} Σ_t e^{-at} ε_LF(m - t) < ∞,   (4.1)

using g_m^{-1} = O(√m). Note 25 only required Σ_m w_m < ∞.

**The first condition holds.** The R-regions carry Balaban's large-field
factor w_m <= exp(-c p(g_m)²) (note 28 §2, (B-small); note 14 (4.3); CMP 102
pp. 257, 267), with p(g) = b_0(1 + log g^{-1})^{p_0} and p_0 > 2. At depth m,
g_m² <= 1/(γ^{-2} + β_* m), so log g_m^{-1} >= ½ log(γ^{-2} + β_* m) and

    w_m <= exp(-c b_0² (1 + ½ log(β_* m))^{2p_0}),

which is smaller than every power of m because 2p_0 > 1. Hence
Σ_m w_m m^{1/2+K/β_*} < ∞ for every K, and the polynomial factor P_m costs
nothing. The second condition is a statement about (LF) and stays open with it.

## 5. Net position of the R-bias core

Generalized averaged H8_Σ ⇐ (LS) + (LF) + (P) + (4.1) (notes 25, 26, 32, 33).

- (LS) is the local, extensive form of (S1)-(S3) in note 29. Its marginal
  part is exact charge conservation through the block average, by Lemma 2.
- The O(g²) entries e and b are the one-fluctuation-integral mechanism of
  note 31, applied to differences of local insertions.
- The only new structural input beyond note 29 is the use of extensive
  norms and the split of Lemma 2.

Because w_m is superpolynomially small (§4), the polynomial factor of
Theorem 3 is harmless. What remains is (LS) and (LF) for Balaban's step.
