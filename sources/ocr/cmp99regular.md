# Spaces of Regular Gauge Field Configurations on a Lattice and Gauge Fixing Conditions 

T. Baiaban*

Abstract. We consider spaces of lattice gauge field configurations satisfying gauge invariant regularity conditions, and intersections of these spaces with a surface given by gauge fixing conditions. We prove that if these conditions are chosen properly then configurations belonging to the intersection are small and regular. In this paper we continue our investigation of the renormalization group method for lattice approximations of gauge field theories. A notion of regularity and spaces of regular gauge field configurations have appeared already in a natural way in [3].
